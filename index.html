About Me

I'm ABHISHEK, a Freelance Data Analyst helping businesses turn messy data into clear dashboards, automated reports, and actionable insights.

I work with Excel, Google Sheets, SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, Looker, Microsoft Fabric, and Databricks to help teams understand performance, spot trends, and make smarter decisions.

From data cleaning and SQL analysis to interactive Power BI and Tableau dashboards, I build practical reporting solutions that save time and improve decision-making.

What I Do

🧹

Data Cleaning & Preparation

I clean, organize, and structure raw business data so it becomes accurate, consistent, and ready for analysis — removing duplicates, fixing errors, standardizing formats, and preparing data from Excel, Google Sheets, SQL databases, and other sources.

🗄️

SQL & Python Analysis

I write SQL queries to extract, join, filter, and analyze data from databases, and use Python for deeper analysis, automation, and repeatable reporting workflows — uncovering trends, patterns, and answers hidden inside your business data.

📊

Power BI, Tableau & Looker Dashboards

I design interactive dashboards that make it easy to track sales, operations, marketing, finance, and other key business metrics. Instead of static reports, you get a clear visual view of what's happening and what needs attention.

📈

Excel & Google Sheets Reporting

I build advanced Excel and Google Sheets reports, trackers, and automated templates — including dynamic dashboards, formulas, pivot tables, charts, and report automation for teams that need simple, practical reporting.

⚙️

Automated Reports & Analytics Support

I reduce manual reporting work by creating automated or semi-automated reporting solutions, and provide ongoing analytics support for businesses that need regular dashboards, monthly reports, or help understanding their data.

🏗️

Modern Data Platforms

I work with tools like Microsoft Fabric and Databricks to support scalable data analysis and reporting workflows — ideal for businesses ready to move beyond basic spreadsheets and build a more structured analytics setup.
